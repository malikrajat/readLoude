Read Aloud with Highlight — manual test page

Load the extension unpacked, open this file, then work through the list.

Checklist
  1. Selection: select one sentence below and press Read.
  2. Article mode: click on empty space (no selection) and press Read. It should read from the first heading.
  3. Highlighting: the blue box should follow the spoken word and stay in sync.
  4. Pause / Resume: pause, wait, resume. It must continue from the same word.
  5. Tab switch: while reading, switch to another tab (it pauses), come back (it resumes).
  6. Manual pause is kept: pause, switch away and back. It must stay paused.
  7. Stop then read again: stop, then start a new selection immediately. No stray "Stopped" flash, no silence.
  8. Reopen the popup while reading: it should show "Reading..." and the Pause/Stop buttons enabled.
  9. Long text (section 6): it must keep going past the 15 second mark.
  10. Unicode (section 7): accents, emoji, right-to-left and CJK should be spoken.
  11. Blocked pages: try chrome://extensions and a new tab. The popup must explain the limitation.
  12. File URL: if this page was opened as file://, enable "Allow access to file URLs" for the extension.
  13. Stop leaves nothing behind: after Stop, run document.querySelectorAll('#read-aloud-selection-marker, .read-aloud-highlight, #read-aloud-floating-highlight').length in the console. It must be 0.
  14. Inline markup (section 4): text split across <strong>, <em> and links must be read as one sentence.

1. Article with headings

Why reading aloud still matters

Reading silently is fast, but reading aloud is how we learn to hear the rhythm of a sentence. When words are spoken, meaning arrives through pace as much as through vocabulary. Studies of early readers show that listening to a text while following it on the page improves both retention and pronunciation.

That is why a simple reading tool is useful far beyond the classroom. Long reports, dense documentation and unfamiliar languages all become easier when something else does the talking and the page keeps your place for you.
